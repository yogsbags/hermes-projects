# Indian origination sources

Use a source only on terms Aveyroni is allowed to access. Name the source on every evidence record.

| Question | Prefer | Treat as a lead only |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legal name, CIN, status | MCA extract, rating report, company footer | Zauba / Tofler / Instafinancials teasers |
| Plants and products | Company site, rating report, pollution or industry directories | Marketplace listings |
| Customers | Company site, OEM award, rating report customer note | "We serve leading OEMs" with no names |
| Exports | IEC-linked shipment records, company export page, rating report | A foreign address on a contact page |
| Revenue band | Rating report, filed financials | Employee-count or "mid-size" guesses |
| Ownership | Filing, rating shareholding, named PE announcement | Founder photo, "family business" slogan |
| Transaction | Named buyer, adviser mandate, stock-exchange disclosure | Rumours, "market chatter" |
| People | Company leadership page, rating report, recent interview with a full name | LinkedIn title alone |

Auto-component clusters to map, not to assume a company sits in: Pune, Chakan, Aurangabad, Sanand, Manesar, Gurugram, Chennai, Hosur, Sriperumbudur, Pantnagar, Pithampur, Bengaluru.
